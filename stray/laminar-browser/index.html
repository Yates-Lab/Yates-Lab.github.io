What is being summed?

The six fitted basis coefficients are expanded into the actual filter at 32 past time lags, covering 4.17–133.33 ms. Color comes from the sum of that time-domain filter. A zero filter has no line. A nonzero filter whose positive and negative parts cancel exactly has zero opacity under this signed-sum display.

The normalization is separate for each session: the strongest absolute sum is fully opaque. It uses all fitted filters in that session, including any whose endpoints lack a usable coordinate. Those missing coordinates and omitted arrows are counted below.

This is an intermediate training snapshot. These signs describe fitted predictive effects on count drive, not identified excitatory or inhibitory synapses. Arrows do not connect different sessions. No quality or edge-strength cutoff was added to the modeled neuron set.

What is in each unit’s details?

STA and STE use recorded Gaborium responses, not model predictions. Each unsmoothed map is shown at its own discrete spatial-variance peak; the map lag may differ slightly from the interpolated latency on the main graph. The original cached RF scale is preserved (arbitrary units). STA colors are centered on zero; STE colors span that unit’s observed values. Each map has its own labeled color scale.

The saccade-triggered trace is this single neuron’s mean recorded firing rate during BackImage, aligned to detected saccade onset. It averages the same complete, continuous, DPI-valid −100 to +300 ms windows at every time point, within individual trials. Overlapping detections are merged; no amplitude or inter-saccade-interval selection is imposed. Nonoverlapping 16.67-ms bins, with no extra smoothing or baseline subtraction. These are descriptive measurements from all available trials, not a model evaluation. Data provenance.

Coordinates, exclusions, and checkpoint

To cover all 30 recordings consistently, coordinates use the existing 240-Hz Gaborium spike-triggered energy caches (20 lags), rather than the earlier 120-Hz archive that covered 20 sessions. Peak spatial variance is interpolated with the same three-point parabola; no latency smoothing is applied. Cache spike totals and response-column ordering are checked against each recording. Depth uses physical channel geometry and waveform localization within each shank. Your manual landmarks are shown where available; increasing depth coordinate is superficial.
